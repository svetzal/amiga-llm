#
# Each glyph is "char X" and then its rows, top first: '#' is ink, '.' is
# paper. Rows 0 to 6 hold the capitals and digits (row 6 is the baseline),
# rows 2 to 6 the lower case, rows 7 and 8 the descenders. A glyph with
# fewer rows than the height is blank below them. The width is the rows'
# length; `gap` blank columns follow every glyph.
